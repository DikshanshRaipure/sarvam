# Product Positioning

## *Necessity for Professionals working in Language bound workspace*

KIVI transforms the way working professionals in language-mandate workspace function.  
KIVI makes day-to-day drafts of documents easier for employees in District courts and for the language teachers for whom typing devnagri on QWERTY is a hectic task.  
KIVI boosts productivity from 20 to 100+ WPM, powered by Sarvam AI models that outperform Gemini Flash 3 and ChatGPT-4o on Indic transcription.  
KIVI turns the most hectic parts of their job into simply talking. It’s no longer a convenience \- it becomes a necessity.  
KIVI will not be the app which will allow the users to download the transcriptions models locally to make KIVI run offline as well.
